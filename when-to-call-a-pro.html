Some surfaces deserve a trained hand.

DIY is great for simple flatwork and light buildup. G&S Exterior Restoration is the better call when access, chemistry, pressure, or surface condition raises the stakes.

Go to gsrestoration.net Compare DIY projects

DIY is reasonable when...

  • The surface is flat, stable, and easy to reach.
  • You can test a hidden area first.
  • You have the right nozzle and detergent.
  • The job is light dirt, pollen, or simple grime.
  • Water runoff can drain safely.

Call G&S when...

  • You need roof, gutter, or second-story cleaning.
  • Siding is oxidized, stained, or near electrical fixtures.
  • Concrete has oil, rust, tire marks, or deep organic staining.
  • Brick mortar, paint, or wood is aged or fragile.
  • You want the job done quickly without risking damage.

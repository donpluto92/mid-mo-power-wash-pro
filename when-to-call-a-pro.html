Professional exterior cleaning for Mid-Missouri properties.

Height, oxidation, fragile materials, specialized stains, drainage, and chemical selection can complicate an exterior cleaning project. G&S Exterior Restoration evaluates the material and chooses the process around its condition.

Evaluate these conditions first

  • Surface material and current condition
  • Type and depth of staining
  • Nearby outlets, fixtures, vents, and seals
  • Safe equipment access and working height
  • Runoff path and nearby landscaping

Professional service is recommended when

  • Siding is oxidized, chalky, loose, or heavily stained.
  • Concrete contains oil, rust, tire marks, or embedded organic growth.
  • Wood, paint, mortar, brick, or pavers show deterioration.
  • The project involves upper-story surfaces or restricted access.
  • Runoff could reach landscaping, electrical components, or neighboring property.

When to call G&S

Know when DIY stops being the smart move.

Some jobs are simple. Others involve height, oxidation, fragile materials, runoff, or chemistry. That is where local exterior-cleaning experience pays for itself.

Visit G&S Exterior RestorationCompare DIY projects

DIY may make sense when...

  • The surface is flat, sound, and easy to reach.
  • You can test a hidden area first.
  • The job is light grime, pollen, or fresh mud.
  • You have the right tip, cleaner, and rinse path.
  • Runoff will not flood, stain, or reach electrical areas.

Call G&S when...

  • You are cleaning roofs, gutters, or second-story siding.
  • Siding is oxidized, streaked, stained, or near electrical fixtures.
  • Concrete has oil, rust, tire marks, or deep organic staining.
  • Brick, paint, wood, or mortar is aged, loose, or fragile.
  • You want the result without gambling on repairs.

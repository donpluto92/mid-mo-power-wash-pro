Project guides

Mid-Missouri exterior cleaning project guides.

Mid-Missouri properties commonly develop algae, pollen, soil buildup, tire marks, and shaded-area staining. Identify the surface, contamination, and runoff path before choosing equipment or cleaner.

Concrete driveways and sidewalks

Remove loose debris and identify oil, rust, tire marks, or organic growth. Pretreat the specific stain, clean in consistent overlapping passes, and direct the final rinse toward a safe drainage area.

Request a driveway cleaning estimate

Siding and house washing

Inspect for oxidation, loose panels, damaged seals, and exposed electrical fixtures. Apply a surface-appropriate detergent at low pressure and rinse downward without forcing water behind the siding.

Review professional help

Wood and composite decks

Confirm whether the surface is wood, composite, or PVC before selecting a cleaner. Use a wide spray pattern and controlled pressure. Stop if wood fibers lift, coatings loosen, or the surface color changes.

Review deck and equipment safety

Brick, stone, and pavers

Inspect mortar, loose material, previous coatings, and joint sand. Test a small area to confirm that the method does not erode joints, discolor masonry, or remove stabilizing sand.

Recommended work order

Complete the inspection before starting the washer.

Protect nearby materials, identify the stain, select the cleaner, establish a drainage path, and confirm the method in a test area.

  1. Protect plants, outlets, lighting, vents, windows, and door gaps.
  2. Identify organic growth, oil, rust, oxidation, or other contamination.
  3. Apply the correct cleaner and follow its labeled dwell time.
  4. Test the rinse method in a small, inconspicuous area.
  5. Complete a controlled final rinse of the surface and nearby property.

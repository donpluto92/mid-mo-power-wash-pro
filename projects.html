Projects

Simple workflows for common outdoor cleaning jobs.

Every project starts with a test spot. If the surface dulls, fuzzes, flakes, or leaks, stop and call a professional.

Concrete driveways and sidewalks

Clear debris, pre-treat stains, use a 25 degree nozzle or surface cleaner, and rinse toward drainage. Avoid lingering in one spot.

Need driveway cleaning help?

Vinyl siding

Use detergent and low pressure. Spray level or downward, never upward under siding laps. Watch windows, vents, outlets, and oxidation.

Check pro-call warning signs

Wood and composite decks

Use a wide fan, low pressure, and long even passes with the grain. Heavy pressure can scar boards or raise wood fibers.

Review deck safety

Brick, stone, and pavers

Inspect mortar and joints first. Older masonry often needs a gentler cleaning plan than a direct pressure pass.

Ask G&S about masonry cleaning
Best order

Clean from delicate to durable.

Start with the lowest-risk areas so you can learn how the machine behaves. Save concrete and heavy grime for after your setup is dialed in.

  1. Protect plants, outlets, lighting, and door gaps.
  2. Apply surface-safe cleaner with low pressure.
  3. Let detergent dwell without drying.
  4. Rinse gently, then increase only if needed.
  5. Final rinse glass, trim, and nearby landscaping.

Tools

Know what each attachment is meant to do.

The right nozzle and accessory can clean faster with less pressure. The wrong one can etch concrete, cut wood fibers, or push water behind siding.

0 degree red nozzle

Very concentrated stream. Avoid for homeowner cleaning because it can damage surfaces and cause serious injury.

15 degree yellow nozzle

Strong cutting angle for tough concrete spots. Use carefully and keep the wand moving.

25 degree green nozzle

A common choice for concrete, patios, and general rinsing when the surface can tolerate pressure.

40 degree white nozzle

Wider fan for siding, decks, painted surfaces, furniture, and safer first test passes.

Soap nozzle

Low-pressure application for detergents. Let the cleaner work before rinsing with a safer fan tip.

Surface cleaner

A round attachment for flatwork that helps reduce striping on driveways, sidewalks, and patios.

Starter kit

What to have before you begin

1

Safety glasses, gloves, long pants, and closed-toe shoes.

2

A pressure washer with readable PSI and GPM ratings.

3

40 and 25 degree nozzles, plus a soap nozzle.

4

Pressure washer detergent matched to the surface.

5

Tarps or water-safe covers for plants, outlets, and delicate items.

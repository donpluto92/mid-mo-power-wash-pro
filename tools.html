Tools and nozzles

Know what the tool is doing before it touches the house.

Nozzle color, spray angle, flow, and distance change the result. The goal is clean surfaces, not the strongest stream.

Red 0 degree tip

A pinpoint stream. Skip it for routine house washing, deck cleaning, and homeowner concrete work unless you fully understand the risk.

Yellow 15 degree tip

Aggressive cleaning angle for stubborn concrete spots. Keep moving and test before using it across a large area.

Green 25 degree tip

Useful for many driveway cleaning and patio jobs when the surface is sound and you need more bite than a rinse.

White 40 degree tip

Wider fan for siding rinses, decks, fences, painted surfaces, and safer first passes.

Black soap nozzle

Low-pressure detergent application. Let the cleaner dwell, then rinse with the right fan tip.

Surface cleaner

A flatwork attachment for driveways and sidewalks. It helps keep concrete cleaning even and reduces wand marks.

Before you start

Set up for control, not speed.

1

Eye protection, gloves, long pants, and closed-toe shoes.

2

A pressure washer with known PSI, GPM, and no leaks.

3

40 degree, 25 degree, and soap tips; leave turbo and red tips aside.

4

Surface-appropriate detergent and a pump sprayer if needed.

5

Covers for outlets, plants, lights, and gaps around doors and windows.

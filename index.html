Quick rule

Start wider, lower, and farther away.

The safest first pass is usually a wider nozzle, lower pressure, and a test spot from a comfortable distance. Move closer only after the surface responds well.

Read the safety basics

Use a pro when you see:

  • Oxidized or chalky siding
  • Roof stains, algae, or steep access
  • Old brick, failing mortar, or loose paint
  • Heavy oil stains or commercial concrete
  • Anything near electrical fixtures or panels

Safety

Treat pressure washers like cutting tools.

A pressure washer can damage skin, eyes, siding, paint, mortar, seals, plants, and electrical components. Respect the spray path every time.

Protect yourself

Wear safety glasses, closed-toe shoes, gloves, and long pants. Use both hands on the wand and keep children and pets away.

Protect the house

Cover outlets, avoid fixtures, keep water out of vents, and do not spray window seals or door thresholds directly.

Protect the surface

Test in a hidden area. If paint flakes, wood fibers lift, mortar crumbles, or siding chalks, stop and switch methods.

Never do this

High-risk pressure washing mistakes

Do not aim at people or animals.

The stream can penetrate skin and drive dirt into tissue.

Do not use the red 0 degree tip for routine cleaning.

It concentrates force into a small point.

Do not pressure wash from a ladder.

Kickback and wet surfaces make falls more likely.

Do not spray electrical equipment.

Water can enter fixtures, outlets, panels, meters, and HVAC components.

Do not blast roofs.

Roof cleaning usually needs professional soft washing and fall protection.

Do not chase stains with force.

Use the right cleaner or call a pro before damaging the surface.
